About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing ideas, reminders and things you want to remember. It helps you keep your notes organized so important thoughts do not disappear.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the note box.
  2. Click the Add Note button.
  3. Review your notes in the notes list.

Features

  • Quick note capture
  • Simple note list
  • Easy navigation
  • Keyboard-friendly controls
